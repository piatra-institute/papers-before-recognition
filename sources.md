## Considered and dropped

- **New York Declaration on Animal Consciousness (2024).** A declaration rather than a peer-reviewed source. The Birch citations carry the same point with a locator.
- **Sloman, the space of possible minds.** Real and relevant to the framing, but the seed cited it to an archive PDF, and no claim in the paper depends on it.
- **Haraway; Deleuze and Guattari.** Listed in the seed's division of philosophical labour. Cut because the paper never uses them: the assemblage material is carried by the boundary-hearing argument, and adding two more names would have made the philosophical section a procession rather than an argument.
- **Hostipitality as a framing.** The seed compared Derridean hostipitality against xenia as interpretive keys. The paper uses Derrida for the aporia and drops the comparison, which belongs to a reading of a television series rather than to this argument.
- **The seed's cultural prologue.** The seed proposed opening with a two-page reading of a horror series whose hospitality infrastructure doubles as an extraction apparatus. Cut entirely. The structure it illustrates (invitation, concealed asymmetry, extraction) is stated directly in one paragraph of "The stranger we made" without needing the example, and a paper that opens on a television plot spends its first two pages establishing that it is serious.
